# Plans and Features

## Overview

The product provides multiple subscription plans designed for different customer needs.

Available plans include:

- Free
- Basic
- Pro
- Enterprise

Each plan may have different features, usage limits, and pricing.

Customers should review the plan information associated with their account before making subscription changes.

## Free Plan

The Free plan is intended for customers who want to use the basic functionality of the product without a paid subscription.

The Free plan may have lower usage limits and fewer advanced features than paid plans.

Customers should review the current plan information for the exact features and limits available to them.

## Basic Plan

The Basic plan provides additional functionality and higher usage limits compared with the Free plan.

It is intended for customers who need more capabilities than the basic free offering.

The exact features and limits should be verified against the current plan configuration.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan provides advanced functionality and higher usage limits for customers with more demanding usage requirements.

Customers on the Pro plan may have access to features that are not available on lower-tier plans.

The exact capabilities and limits should be verified against the customer's current plan information.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is designed for organizations with larger usage requirements and more advanced business needs.

Enterprise customers may have access to additional capabilities, higher limits, and organization-level functionality.

Customer-specific Enterprise features should be verified using the customer's account information.

## Plan Comparison

The general plan hierarchy is:

Free → Basic → Pro → Enterprise

Moving to a higher plan may provide additional features or higher limits.

Moving to a lower plan may reduce available features or limits.

The plan hierarchy should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every feature is available on every plan.

## Plan Pricing

Subscription pricing can depend on the selected plan and billing cycle.

Customers may be billed monthly or annually.

The actual price for a customer must be retrieved from the subscription information associated with their account.

Support agents must not guess or invent customer-specific pricing.

## Usage Limits

Different plans may have different usage limits.

Examples of limits may include:

- Number of users
- API usage
- Storage
- Report generation
- Data processing
- Advanced functionality

Customers should check the current plan documentation for the applicable limits.

## Upgrading

Customers can upgrade to a higher plan when they need additional functionality or higher limits.

After a successful upgrade, the new plan's applicable features and limits should become available.

If payment succeeds but the new plan does not become active, support should investigate the subscription status.

## Downgrading

Customers can request a downgrade to a lower plan.

Before downgrading, customers should verify that their current usage fits within the limits of the new plan.

Downgrading may remove access to features or reduce usage limits.

## Changing Billing Cycle

Customers may be able to change between monthly and annual billing.

Changing the billing cycle can affect:

- Billing frequency
- Renewal date
- Amount charged
- Future subscription payments

Customers should review the applicable billing terms before confirming the change.

## Plan Availability

Not every feature is necessarily available to every customer.

Availability may depend on:

- Subscription plan
- Account configuration
- Organization settings
- Product version
- Enabled features

When a customer asks whether a specific feature is available, support should first identify the general plan requirements and then verify the customer's actual subscription.

## Customer-Specific Plan Questions

General questions such as:

> "What is the Pro plan?"

> "What plans are available?"

> "What is the difference between Free and Pro?"

can be answered using the knowledge base.

Customer-specific questions such as:

> "What plan am I currently on?"

> "How much am I paying?"

> "When does my plan renew?"

must be answered using the customer's subscription and billing information.

## Important Support Rule

The knowledge base describes general plan capabilities.

Customer-specific subscription information must always come from the customer's actual subscription record.

The support agent must not assume a customer's plan based on their question or previous conversation.
