# Subscriptions

## Overview

Customers can subscribe to different plans based on their organization's needs.

Each subscription has:

- A subscription plan
- A subscription status
- A billing cycle
- A monthly or equivalent price
- A start date
- A renewal date

Customers can view their current subscription information from their account.

## Subscription Status

A subscription may have one of the following statuses:

- Active
- Cancelled
- Paused
- Pending

An active subscription provides access to the features and limits associated with the customer's plan.

A cancelled subscription may remain available until the end of its applicable billing period.

## Changing Plans

Customers can change their subscription plan from the subscription section of their account.

Customers may:

- Upgrade to a higher plan
- Downgrade to a lower plan
- Change their billing cycle
- Cancel their subscription

Before changing plans, customers should review the features, usage limits, and pricing associated with the new plan.

## Upgrading a Subscription

When a customer upgrades their plan, the new plan's features and limits become available after the upgrade has been successfully processed.

If a customer has completed payment but the new plan has not activated, the issue should be investigated by support.

Customers should not repeatedly submit payments if an upgrade appears to be stuck.

## Downgrading a Subscription

Downgrading a subscription may reduce the features, usage limits, or capabilities available to the customer.

Customers should review the destination plan before confirming a downgrade.

Existing data may be affected if it exceeds the limits of the new plan.

Customers should contact support if they are unsure how a downgrade will affect their account.

## Billing Cycle

Subscriptions support monthly and annual billing cycles.

The billing cycle determines how frequently the customer is charged.

Customers can view their current billing cycle and renewal date in their subscription details.

Changing the billing cycle may affect when the next charge occurs.

## Subscription Renewal

An active subscription normally renews according to its billing cycle.

Before renewal, customers should ensure that their payment method is valid and has sufficient funds or available credit.

If the renewal payment fails, the customer may need to update the payment method and retry the transaction.

## Failed Renewal

A subscription renewal may fail because of:

- Insufficient funds
- Card decline
- Expired card
- Incorrect payment information
- Payment gateway errors
- Bank transfer failure
- Security or fraud checks

Customers should first verify their payment method and then retry the payment when appropriate.

If repeated payment attempts fail, the issue should be escalated to support.

## Cancelling a Subscription

Customers can request cancellation from their subscription settings.

Before cancelling, customers should review the cancellation terms associated with their subscription.

Depending on the subscription terms, access may remain available until the end of the current billing period.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically mean that a previously completed payment will be refunded.

Refund requests are handled separately according to the applicable refund policy.

## Reactivating a Subscription

If a subscription has been cancelled but remains eligible for reactivation, the customer may be able to reactivate it from their subscription settings.

If the reactivation option is unavailable, support should review the customer's subscription status.

## Subscription Information

Support agents can help customers understand:

- Current plan
- Subscription status
- Billing cycle
- Renewal date
- Plan features
- Plan limits
- Cancellation status

Customer-specific subscription information must be retrieved from the customer data system rather than guessed from general documentation.

## Important Support Rule

General subscription documentation explains how subscriptions work.

It does not contain customer-specific information.

For questions such as:

> "What plan am I currently on?"

> "When does my subscription renew?"

> "Is my subscription active?"

the support agent must retrieve the customer's actual subscription information from the customer database.
