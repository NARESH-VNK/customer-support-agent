# Billing and Payments

## Overview

Customers are billed according to the billing cycle and subscription plan associated with their account. Billing information, payment status, transaction history, and invoices are available through the billing section of the customer account.

The system supports monthly and annual billing cycles.

## Payment Methods

Customers can use supported payment methods available in their account's billing settings.

Customers should keep their payment information up to date to avoid failed subscription renewals.

Customers should never share their complete card number, CVV, password, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials with support agents.

## Payment Processing

When a customer makes a payment, the transaction may have one of several statuses:

- Successful
- Failed
- Pending
- Refunded
- Partially refunded

A successful transaction means the payment was completed successfully.

A failed transaction means the payment could not be completed.

A pending transaction means the payment is still being processed.

A refunded transaction means the payment was returned to the customer.

A partially refunded transaction means only part of the original transaction amount was returned.

## Failed Payments

A payment may fail for several reasons, including:

- Insufficient funds
- Card declined
- Expired card
- Incorrect card details
- Payment gateway errors
- Bank transfer failure
- Security or fraud checks

If a payment fails, customers should first verify their payment method and billing information.

For card payments, customers should confirm that:

1. The card has not expired.
2. The card details are correct.
3. The billing information is correct.
4. Sufficient funds or available credit are available.
5. The card provider allows the transaction.

Customers should avoid repeatedly submitting a payment when a payment service is experiencing an error.

## Payment Gateway Errors

A payment gateway error means that the payment service was unable to successfully process the transaction.

If a gateway error occurs, the customer should wait briefly and retry the payment.

If the problem continues, the issue should be escalated to support for investigation.

## Billing Cycles

Subscriptions can use either monthly or annual billing.

The billing cycle determines how frequently the customer is charged.

Customers can view their current billing cycle and renewal date in their subscription information.

Changing a billing cycle may affect the timing and amount of future charges.

## Invoices

Invoices are associated with successful subscription billing transactions.

Customers can access available invoices from the billing section of their account.

If an invoice is missing or cannot be downloaded, the customer should contact support.

## Updating Payment Information

Customers can update their payment method from the billing settings of their account.

When replacing an expired or invalid card, customers should update the payment method before the next subscription renewal.

After updating the payment method, a failed transaction may be retried according to the account's billing process.

## Unauthorized Transactions

If a customer sees a transaction they do not recognize, they should contact support immediately.

Support may investigate the transaction and associated account activity.

Customers should never provide their full card number, CVV, password, or authentication codes through support conversations.

## Important Security Rule

Support agents must never ask customers to provide:

- Full payment card numbers
- CVV or security codes
- Account passwords
- Authentication codes
- Private API keys
- Other authentication secrets

Sensitive credentials should never be stored in support ticket messages.
